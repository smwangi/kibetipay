# Disputes, reversals and security (sample policy)

## Sent money to the wrong person
1. Contact support within 24 hours with the transfer ID.
2. We ask the recipient to approve a reversal. Completed transfers cannot be reversed without the recipient's consent
   or a court order.
3. Reversals appear as a new entry on your statement; the original entry is never deleted.

## Rejected transfers
A transfer can be rejected by our automated risk checks, for example for unusually large amounts, many transfers in a
short time, or sending most of your balance at once. No money leaves your wallet when a transfer is rejected.
You can try again later or contact support.

## Unusual activity alerts
If your spending is far above your normal pattern we may show an unusual-activity alert. If you did not make the
transaction, change your password immediately and contact support.

## Security
- KibetiPay staff will never ask for your password or PIN.
- Use a unique password and enable two-factor authentication when available.
