# Loans (sample policy for the KibetiPay learning project)

## How to get a loan
Loans are offered through registered KibetiPay agents. An agent submits the application for you; you need an
active KibetiPay wallet. Decisions are usually automatic within a minute. Some applications are reviewed by our
credit team.

## Products
- Soko 4-week loan: KES 500 to KES 50,000, repaid in 4 weekly installments. Interest 10% flat plus a 3% fee.
- Biashara 3-month loan: KES 5,000 to KES 200,000, repaid in 3 monthly installments. Interest 24% flat plus a 3% fee.

## Loan limits
First-time borrowers start with a smaller limit (KES 5,000 for Soko). Each loan you repay in full raises the amount
you can borrow next time, up to twice your largest repaid loan. You can only have one open loan at a time.

## Repaying
Repay from your wallet in the app (top up via M-Pesa first) or hand cash to your agent, who records it.
Payments go first to any penalty, then fees, then interest, then principal, oldest installment first.

## Late payment
A late installment attracts a small daily penalty. Total penalties on an installment can never exceed its principal.
Loans more than 90 days late are classified as defaulted, which prevents future borrowing.
