# Fees and limits (sample policy for the KibetiPay learning project)

## Transfer fees
- Transfers between KibetiPay wallets are free up to KES 1,000.
- KES 1,001 to KES 10,000: flat fee of KES 15.
- KES 10,001 to KES 100,000: flat fee of KES 30.
- Deposits are free. (Fees are not yet charged by the ledger; see roadmap phase 3.)

## Limits
- Maximum single transfer: KES 100,000.
- Maximum single deposit: KES 150,000.
- Wallet balance must never go below zero.

## Processing time
- Most transfers complete within a few seconds.
- Some transfers are held for manual review by our risk team. Review normally completes within 2 business hours.
  While a transfer is in review, the money has not left your wallet.
